1. **Kernel necessity.** Every non-degenerate theorem-bearing proof/refutation regime instantiates the kernel roles: admissibility, standing, reference, and irreversibility / final consequence.
2. **Ambient counterexamplehood.** An ambient ZFC / classical-analysis counterexample is theorem-bearing construction, not a bare expression; it requires admissible formation, proof-standing, fixed reference, same-target fidelity, and final theorem consequence.
3. **Fefferman proof/refutation regime.** Fefferman periodic statement (B) induces a fixed proof/refutation regime over the official datum class, equation, carrier, pressure gauge, continuation question, and endpoint.

5. **Counterexample soundness.** A finite-horizon obstruction refutes Fefferman (B) only if it completes as a licit same-scope endpoint condition of the official proof/refutation regime.
6. **Endpoint-status exhaustion.** The realized-lineage trichotomy exhausts the endpoint statuses: standing-positive admissible witness, terminal standing failure, or illicit same-scope gate / scope move.
7. **Branch elimination and official endpoint.** The non-witness branches collapse, the standing-positive witness branch is forced into excluded branch data, and no same-scope finite-horizon counterexample completes.
