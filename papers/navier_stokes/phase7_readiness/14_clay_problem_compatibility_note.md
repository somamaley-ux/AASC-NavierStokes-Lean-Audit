# Clay problem compatibility and method-neutrality note

The manuscript's endpoint is Fefferman periodic statement (B). Its proof class is a constraint-formalism impossibility proof, not a PDE-native continuation-estimate proof.

Clay's rules require a complete mathematical solution to the official problem description, publication in a qualifying outlet, at least two years of public mathematical examination, general acceptance, and later CMI evaluation. They do not require a conventional PDE proof template. Fefferman's statement gives solvers reasonable leeway while retaining the heart of the problem.

The correct stress-test target is therefore not the absence of a new vortex-stretching estimate. The correct target is whether the non-optional fixed-scope kernel applies to the official periodic regime and whether finite-horizon endpoint conditions are exhausted.
